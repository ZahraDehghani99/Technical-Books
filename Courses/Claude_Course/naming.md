## Why is it called Claude?

**Claude** is widely understood to be named after **Claude Shannon (1916–2001)**, the American mathematician, electrical engineer, and computer scientist known as the **“father of information theory.”**

Shannon's 1948 work, *A Mathematical Theory of Communication*, established the foundations for understanding how information can be represented, transmitted, stored, and processed. His work introduced fundamental concepts such as **bits, information entropy, data compression, and communication over noisy channels**.

Shannon also made important contributions to early computing and AI, including experiments with machine learning and a maze-solving electromechanical mouse called **Theseus**.

The connection to an AI assistant is therefore particularly fitting:

> **Claude Shannon → Information Theory → Computing → AI**

Anthropic's Claude is not directly based on Shannon's work, nor did Shannon invent modern AI or LLMs. Rather, the name is generally seen as a **tribute to a foundational figure in information and computing**.
